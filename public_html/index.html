HOW WE MINISTER

Six unique ministry practices

01

CALLING

Called by God to be His "ambassadors" as well as "salt" and "light" in the marketplace.

02

PRAYER

By developing intimacy with Christ, we seek His favor and power in reaching the lost.

03

TEAMS

Effective teams of business and professional men and women who support one another.

04

EVANGELISM

Compelled by the love of God to bring reconciliation to humanity through the Gospel.

05

DISCIPLESHIP

In humility and love we help each other become authentic Christ-followers.

06

LEADERSHIP

Servant leadership, as modeled by Jesus Christ, guides how we conduct ourselves.
