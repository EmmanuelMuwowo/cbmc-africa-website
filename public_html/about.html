ABOUT US

Who we are

We are Connecting Business & the Marketplace to Christ — an interdenominational, evangelical Christian organization comprised of national associations around Africa.

OUR MISSION

Strengthening teams to reach the marketplace.

Our mission is to establish and strengthen CBMC national and regional teams to be effective as they present the Gospel of Jesus Christ to their colleagues in the global marketplace — and to help others be transformed to Christ-likeness.

Jesus Christ + The Marketplace = CBMC

Jesus compared the kingdom of God to a mustard seed that grows larger than all the garden plants. Today authentic Christ-followers live and work in every major city on earth — enabling the next step in that growth: marketplace ministry.

WHAT WE DO

Three guiding initiatives

🙏
INITIATIVE ONE

Prayer

Prayer is our first priority — the key to our vital life in Christ. We pray with and for one another so lives come to know the Lord.

🌱
INITIATIVE TWO

Growing the Ministry

We long to see every country in Africa have a CBMC presence — laborers in the marketplace taking Jesus to people right where they are.

🤝
INITIATIVE THREE

Fellowship (C3)

Connect3 teams give business people a place to connect to God and one another, meeting weekly in cities across the continent.

Our six ministry distinctives

01

CALLING

Called by God to be His "ambassadors" as well as "salt" and "light" in the marketplace.

02

PRAYER

By developing intimacy with Christ, we seek His favor and power in reaching the lost.

03

TEAMS

Effective teams of business and professional men and women who support one another.

04

EVANGELISM

Compelled by the love of God to bring reconciliation to humanity through the Gospel.

05

DISCIPLESHIP

In humility and love we help each other become authentic Christ-followers.

06

LEADERSHIP

Servant leadership, as modeled by Jesus Christ, guides how we conduct ourselves.
