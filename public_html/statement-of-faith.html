ABOUT US

Statement of Faith

The core beliefs shared across the CBMC movement.

01

We believe in one God, eternally existing in three persons: Father, Son and Holy Spirit.

02

We believe the Scriptures of the Old and New Testament are verbally inspired by God, and inerrant in the original writings, and that they are of supreme and final authority in faith and life.

03

We believe that man was created in the image of God, that he sinned, and that all people are born with a sinful nature that separates them from God.

04

We believe the Lord Jesus Christ died for our sins according to the Scriptures, was buried, and rose again on the third day.

05

We believe that all who receive by faith the Lord Jesus Christ are born again of the Holy Spirit and thereby become children of God.

An interdenominational, evangelical Christian organization comprised of national associations around Africa.
