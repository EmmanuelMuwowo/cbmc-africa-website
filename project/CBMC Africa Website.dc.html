ABOUT US

Who we are

We are Connecting Business & the Marketplace to Christ — an interdenominational, evangelical Christian organization comprised of national associations around Africa.

OUR MISSION

Strengthening teams to reach the marketplace.

Our mission is to establish and strengthen CBMC national and regional teams to be effective as they present the Gospel of Jesus Christ to their colleagues in the global marketplace — and to help others be transformed to Christ-likeness.

Jesus Christ + The Marketplace = CBMC

Jesus compared the kingdom of God to a mustard seed that grows larger than all the garden plants. Today authentic Christ-followers live and work in every major city on earth — enabling the next step in that growth: marketplace ministry.
